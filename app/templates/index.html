About the project

This site tracks late third-period comebacks that force overtime or shootouts. We log the lead, time of the tying goal, and contextual notes to aid analysis.

We do not claim any wrongdoing. The goal is to document trends for fans, journalists, and analysts to review.

Unaffiliated with the NHL, teams, or sportsbooks. Data sourced from public feeds.
